About FloodLens

Spatial index Uber H3 Resolution 9 (~174 m hex aperture, ~0.1 km²)
Study coverage 41,703 cells covering Delhi, Gurugram, and Noida
Terrain weights HAND (25%), TWI (25%), Flow Acc (20%), Built-up (15%), Underpass (10%), Depression (5%)
Rainfall threshold Ri = Rref · exp(-K · Si)
Evaluation protocol Fixed protocol on 17 news-verified waterlogging spots (14 corridors, 3 points)
Planning disclaimer: FloodLens produces a relative susceptibility ranking designed for corridor prioritization and pump pre-positioning. It operates at approximately 1 km spatial aggregation and does not simulate dynamic hydrodynamic water depths or storm sewer pipe hydraulics.
